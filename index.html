Straight from the distillery

The grades

Three oils, one standard: pure

  • 01 Original Churon OudOur rarest oil, from the most resin-rich Churon wood. Offered to regular buyers first. 3–4 batches a year
  • 02 Luha Churon OudChuron-grade oil with a deep, rich character, made in small batches. Limited
  • 03 Classic Agar OudOur everyday oud oil and the one most resellers start with. Steady supply for repeat orders. Year-round
  • 04 Agarwood chipsNatural chips for burning and bakhoor. Ask for current grades and photos. On request

Made in our own factory

Oud oil comes from the dark resin an agar tree forms to heal itself. We grow the trees, cut and sort the wood by hand, and distill every batch ourselves in Sujanagar. Nothing is added at any stage.

  1. 01Resin forms in cultivated agar trees
  2. 02Wood is cut and sorted by hand
  3. 03Slow distillation in our own factory
  4. 04Oil settles, then is bottled and packed
