Chapter I · The family

Fifty years in the agar village of Bangladesh.

II

Chapter II · Tree to oil

How the oil is made.

Oud oil comes from the dark resin an agar tree forms to heal itself. In Moulvibazar, growers help the tree along the traditional way.

  1. The tree

    Iron nails are set into cultivated agar trees. Over the following years the tree forms fragrant resin around each wound.

  2. The harvest

    The resinous wood is cut, and our team separates it by hand. The darkest, richest pieces become Churon.

  3. The still

    The wood is distilled slowly in our own factory in Sujanagar. Nothing is added at any stage.

  4. The bottle

    The oil is left to settle, then bottled. Samples and wholesale orders are packed in our factory.

V

Questions buyers ask

Straight answers.

How can I check the oil is pure?

Order a sample first and judge it yourself. You are welcome to visit the factory and watch the distillation. For orders from 500 ml, we arrange an independent lab test before shipping.

Is the wood cultivated or wild?

Our wood comes from our own agar trees and from local farmers and growers we know. Our Classic grade comes from cultivated trees, with resin formed by the traditional iron-nail method.

Why are the Churon grades limited?

Churon wood is rare. We can distill Churon oil only 3 or 4 times a year, while Classic Agar Oud is made all year. Message us to reserve from the next batch.

What is the minimum order?

Samples come in 6 ml and 12 ml. Wholesale starts at 120 g, which is about 10 tola.

Why are prices not listed?

Each Churon batch is different and prices change with grade and quantity. We quote every order, usually the same day.

Do you ship to my country?

We ship to the Middle East, the UK, the USA, France and other countries. Tell us your city and we will confirm the method, cost and paperwork for your order.
